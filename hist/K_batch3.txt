诏安八仙茶|Zhao'an Baxian was selected and bred by Zhao'an growers from 1965 onwards and is one of Fujian's modern elite hybrids.
漳平水仙饼|Zhangping's pressed-cake Shuixian is the only cake-form oolong in China and has been produced since the Qing in Nanyang town.
龙岩斜背茶|Longyan Xiebei is a historic Fujian green with Qing-era records.
福安坦洋工夫|Tanyang Gongfu was created in 1851 in Tanyang village, Fu'an, and is the ancestor of the "three Fujian gongfu" historic black teas.
