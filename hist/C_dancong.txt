@dancong|Local tradition says the fleeing Southern Song emperor Zhao Bing passed Phoenix Mountain thirsty, was given tea by the She people, and named it "Song tea". The earliest written record is in a Ming-dynasty Chaozhou Prefectural Gazetteer, which lists leaf and bud tea from Raoping among the tribute taxes. Phoenix farmers picked, processed and sold the best trees one bush at a time, each under its own name, which is where "dancong" (single bush) comes from; more than ten thousand old trees are still managed this way, and over 80 aroma types are now recognised. The Phoenix Dancong Ancient Tree Catalogue counts 4,682 trees over 200 years old. In the hard years of the 1960s many gardens were cleared for sweet potatoes and peanuts, and planting recovered in the 1980s. Strain names come from the aroma, the leaf, the shape of the tree, where the mother tree stands, or the era it was named in.

乌岽宋种单丛|"Song strain" means a variety surviving from the Song dynasty, and is the most prized class of Dancong. The best known, Song Strain No. 1, grew at about 1,150 m near Lizaiping village in Wudong. Legend credits the Li family, who settled there in the late Southern Song, with finding it. Villagers first called it Tuan Shu Ye after its leaf; it was sold as Yan Shang Zhen in 1946, named Huang Zhi Xiang (gardenia) by the Wudong cooperative in 1956, and called Dongfang Hong from 1969 before the name Song Zhong was restored in 1980. Villagers spread it by cuttings from 1962. The mother tree, over 700 years old, died in 2016 and is kept at the Chaozhou Phoenix Dancong Tea Museum.
东方红单丛|Dongfang Hong ("The East Is Red") was the name given from spring 1969 to Song Strain No. 1, the 700-year-old Song-strain mother tree of Lizaiping in Wudong, and it is still the most common alias for that strain. The name Song Zhong was officially restored in 1980.
团树叶单丛|Tuan Shu Ye was the villagers' first name for Song Strain No. 1 at Lizaiping in Wudong, because its leaf looked like that of the tuan tree. The same strain was later called Huang Zhi Xiang, Dongfang Hong and Song Zhong.
大庵宋种单丛|The mother tree stands in Da'an (Dayan) village, Phoenix town, and is around 500 to 600 years old. It yields only about 13 jin of tea a year.
