@ttes|Taiwan's modern tea cultivars come from the Tea Research and Extension Station (茶業改良場), which numbers each release "TTES No." with the year it was named. In the Japanese colonial period Taiwan grew four base cultivars, Qingxin Oolong, Qingxin Damao, Dayeh Oolong and Yingzhi Hongxin, and the colonial government brought in Assam large-leaf seed from India, which only took at Sun Moon Lake. The station's crosses of these with Indian, Burmese, Thai and Keemun stock have produced 25 numbered cultivars since 1969.
