花果山云雾茶|Huaguoshan Yunwu has been produced for more than 900 years and was once a tribute tea for the imperial court. In 1934 it was named one of Jiangsu's three great teas.
二泉银毫|Erquan Yinhao was created in 1986.
前峰雪莲|One of Jiangsu's new famous teas of the 1980s, Qianfeng Xuelian was awarded the title of national famous tea at the 1989 national tea assessment.
茅山青峰|Production began in the 1940s, when the tea was called Qiqiang ("flag and spear"). It took its present name, Maoshan Qingfeng, in 1983.
荆溪云片|Jingxi Yunpian was created in 1986, passed Wuxi city appraisal in 1989 and the same year received the Ministry of Agriculture's title of national famous tea.
无锡毫茶|In 1986 the Ministry of Commerce named Wuxi Haocha one of China's national famous teas, and in 1989 it won the title again at the Ministry of Agriculture's quality awards in Xi'an.
古劳茶|Gulao tea is said to have been created by Hakka people in the Song dynasty. A local saying runs: "Before there was a Heshan county, there was Gulao tea."
荔枝红茶|The earliest records of lychee black tea are in newspapers from the middle and late Republican era.
合箩茶|Heluo tea from Xinyi was presented as a tribute tea in the Qianlong reign of the Qing.
金秀野生红茶|This source counts Jinxiu among the 42 tea-producing areas named in Lu Yu's Tang-dynasty Classic of Tea.
